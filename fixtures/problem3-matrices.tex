\section*{Problem 3: Matrix Products}

Let
\[ A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix} \]
and
\[ B = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 6 \\ 7 & 8 \end{pmatrix}. \]
Compute the products $AB$ and $BA$, and compare the two results.

\section*{Solution}

\textbf{Step 1.} The $(1,1)$ entry of $AB$ combines row 1 of the first
matrix with column 1 of the second:
\[
(AB)_{11} = 1 \cdot 5 + 2 \cdot 7 = 19.
\]

\textbf{Step 2.} Computing the other three entries the same way:
\[
AB = \begin{pmatrix} 19 & 22 \\ 43 & 50 \end{pmatrix}
\]
and
\[
BA = \begin{pmatrix} 23 & 34 \\ 31 & 46 \end{pmatrix}.
\]

\textbf{Step 3.} The two products are different: $AB \neq BA$.

\section*{Answer}

\[
AB = \begin{pmatrix} 19 & 22 \\ 43 & 50 \end{pmatrix}
\neq
\begin{pmatrix} 23 & 34 \\ 31 & 46 \end{pmatrix}
= BA.
\]
