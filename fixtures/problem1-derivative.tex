\section*{Problem 1: Differentiation}

Differentiate the function
\[ f(x) = \frac{2x+1}{x^{2}+3} \]
and simplify the result.

\section*{Solution}

\textbf{Step 1.} The function is a quotient, so apply the quotient rule
with $u = 2x+1$ and $v = x^{2}+3$:
\[
f'(x) = \frac{2(x^{2}+3) - (2x+1) \cdot 2x}{(x^{2}+3)^{2}}.
\]

\textbf{Step 2.} Expand the numerator:
\[
2x^{2} + 6 - 4x^{2} - 2x = -2x^{2} - 2x + 6.
\]

\textbf{Step 3.} Factor $-2$ out of the numerator:
\[
f'(x) = \frac{-2(x^{2}+x-3)}{(x^{2}+3)^{2}}.
\]

\section*{Answer}

\[
f'(x) = \frac{-2(x^{2}+x-3)}{(x^{2}+3)^{2}}.
\]
